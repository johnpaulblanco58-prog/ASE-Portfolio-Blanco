# Course Introduction

## Exploring Agentic Software Engineering and Responsible AI

Agentic Software Engineering explores the integration of artificial
intelligence agents into modern software development. It focuses on how
intelligent agents can interpret information, make decisions, perform
tasks, and work toward specific objectives with varying levels of
autonomy.

This course introduces key concepts and practices related to the
development of AI-powered systems, including software engineering
principles, intelligent agents, Agile development, Scrum, and version
control using Git. It also examines how agentic systems differ from
traditional software applications, particularly in their ability to
analyze situations, adapt to changing information, and select actions to
achieve goals.

In addition to understanding the capabilities of AI agents, the course
emphasizes responsible AI development. Topics such as ethical
considerations, privacy, security, AI-related risks, and human oversight
are important in ensuring that autonomous systems are reliable, safe,
and accountable. Through these topics, students develop an understanding
of how agentic technologies can support modern software development and
help address real-world problems responsibly.
