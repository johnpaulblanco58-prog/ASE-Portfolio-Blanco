# Personal Learning Goals

Throughout the course **Agentic Software Engineering**, I aim to develop
the knowledge and practical skills needed to understand, evaluate, and
responsibly use AI-powered agentic systems. My personal learning goals
are:

1.  **Understand Agentic Software Engineering.** Learn the fundamental
    concepts of agentic systems and how intelligent agents perceive
    information, make decisions, and perform actions to achieve specific
    objectives.

2.  **Differentiate traditional and agentic systems.** Understand how
    AI-powered agents differ from conventional software applications in
    terms of autonomy, adaptability, decision-making, and task
    execution.

3.  **Develop collaborative software engineering skills.** Improve my
    ability to use Git and GitHub for version control, manage project
    changes, and collaborate effectively with other developers.

4.  **Apply Agile development practices.** Understand how Agile methods
    and the Scrum framework support iterative development, teamwork,
    feedback, testing, and continuous improvement.

5.  **Recognize responsible AI considerations.** Develop awareness of
    ethical concerns, privacy, security, and potential risks associated
    with autonomous AI agents.

6.  **Value human oversight and accountability.** Understand why human
    review, supervision, and responsible decision-making remain
    important when designing and deploying AI-enabled applications.

By achieving these goals, I hope to build a stronger foundation in
Agentic Software Engineering and become more capable of developing or
working with AI systems that are useful, reliable, secure, and
responsible.
